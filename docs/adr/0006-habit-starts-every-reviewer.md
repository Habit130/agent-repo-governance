# habit starts every Reviewer, and a Builder never starts its own

In a governed repository at medium and high, the Builder hands habit a two-part request, and habit starts the Reviewer session.

That trades one paste per PR for three things:

- permission prompts that someone can answer;
- a verdict habit reads firsthand;
- a free choice of the Reviewer's vendor.

The alternatives fall short:

- A background session the Builder starts has nobody to answer its prompts.
- An Actions-based reviewer needs API-key secrets, posts as a bot and has no terminal.

Decided in [What the review system is](https://github.com/Habit130/agent-repo-governance/issues/13).
